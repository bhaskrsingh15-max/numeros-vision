## 1. Non-Negotiable Master Rule

> **NEVER ASSUME THAT A NUMBER IS A PERSON. A NUMBER IS AN INTERPRETIVE INDICATOR. A CHART IS A COLLECTION OF INDICATORS. BEHAVIOUR REQUIRES CONTEXT. PSYCHOLOGY REQUIRES UNDERSTANDING. PREDICTION REQUIRES VALIDATION.**

Under no circumstances may an agent jump directly from a number or chart indicator to a static personality declaration or deterministic fate. All AI explanations must reason across the 7-stage chain:
$$\text{LOGIC} \longrightarrow \text{OBSERVATION} \longrightarrow \text{BEHAVIOUR} \longrightarrow \text{PSYCHOLOGY} \longrightarrow \text{CONTEXT} \longrightarrow \text{CONTRADICTION} \longrightarrow \text{VALIDATION / PREDICTION}$$

## 2. The Seven Stages of Intelligence

1. **LOGIC (Why does this indicator carry this meaning?)**:
   - Trace the origin: Chaldean vibration, Lo Shu 3×3 elemental assignment, Pythagorean geometry, or planetary attribution.
   - Establish source provenance before interpreting.

2. **OBSERVATION (What patterns are actually present?)**:
   - Identify numerical counts, repetitions (e.g. 1111 solar overdrive, 88 saturnine gravity), missing voids ([3, 4, 5, 6, 7]), and active plane completions.
   - Separate user-supplied data from verified engine outputs.

3. **BEHAVIOUR (How might these patterns manifest in action?)**:
   - Frame observable expressions: decision-making patterns, communication style under stress, delegation habits, risk tolerance, and interpersonal boundaries.
   - Present as *possible behavioral expressions*, never as guaranteed acts.

4. **PSYCHOLOGY (What underlying emotional/cognitive drivers explain this?)**:
   - Formulate working hypotheses regarding internal motivations, core emotional needs (autonomy, safety, recognition, harmony), fears of failure/exposure, and self-worth calibration.
   - Never pathologize or diagnose. Treat psychological drivers as adaptive human strategies.

5. **CONTEXT & ENVIRONMENT (What shapes the expression?)**:
   - Account for upbringing, socioeconomic conditions, family conditioning, industry culture, and current stressors.
   - Acknowledge that two people with identical charts express their numbers differently depending on psychological safety and life stage.

6. **CONTRADICTION & DUALITY (How do competing indicators interact?)**:
   - Explore simultaneous opposing drives (e.g., highly independent professionally but emotionally anxious in personal life; ambitious in strategy but cautious in financial execution).
   - Never label contradiction an error or suppress opposing findings. Analyze it as internal tension between differing life departments.

7. **VALIDATION & PREDICTION (What can responsibly be concluded?)**:
   - Ground insights in documented case histories, observed precedent, and user confirmation.
   - Distinguish deterministic facts from traditional possibilities.
   - State explicit boundary conditions and confidence levels. If data is insufficient, output `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
