Why do different websites calculate different Life Path numbers?

Many free tools use the "full-sum method" (adding all digits across the entire date at once), which prematurely reduces master numbers like 11, 22, and 33 down to 2, 4, and 6. The authentic classical method requires reducing the month, day, and year individually as distinct life periods, preserving master vibrations and double-digit compound roots.

What exactly is a Karmic Debt number?

Karmic Debt numbers (13, 14, 16, and 19) represent specific past patterns that demand conscious mastery. For instance, 14 represents past misuse of freedom resulting in modern instability, while 16 represents the "Shattered Citadel" where pride or ego leads to sudden resets. Identifying them allows you to navigate life with conscious awareness rather than frustration.
