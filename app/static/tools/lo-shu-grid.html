✦ Direct Answer Definition · Classical Numerology Framework

The Lo Shu Grid is an ancient 3×3 numerological magic square that maps the digits of a person's full birth date across 8 energetic planes (mental, emotional, practical, thought, will, action, and diagonal success yogas). In numerology, every row, column, and diagonal sums to 15, revealing strengths, amplified vibrations, and missing numbers that guide remedial harmonization.

5-Step Calculation Method:
  1. Date of Birth: Write down your full birth date in DD/MM/YYYY format.
  2. Birth Number (Mulank): Reduce your calendar day of birth to a single digit (1–9).
  3. Destiny Number (Bhagyank): Sum all digits of your date, month, and year to a single digit (1–9).
  4. Plot the Numbers: Place all digits of your birth date, Mulank, and Bhagyank into the traditional 3×3 matrix: Top row (4-9-2), Middle row (3-5-7), Bottom row (8-1-6). The digit 0 is omitted as it has no cell in the classical grid.
  5. Assess the Planes: Identify completed planes (such as 4-5-6 Golden Rajyog or 2-5-8 Silver Success Plane) and note any missing numbers.
