How Favourable Numbers Work: Method & Plain-Words Formula

In Vedic and Lo Shu numerology, "favourable numbers" are not random lottery picks—they are deterministic single-digit indicators derived directly from your Gregorian birth date and the current calendar year:

  • Mulank (Birth Day number): Digital Root(Birth Day). Born on the 24th → 2 + 4 = 6 (Shukra / Venus).
  • Bhagyank (Life Path number): Digital Root(DD + MM + YYYY). Born on 24-10-1993 → 2 + 4 + 1 + 0 + 1 + 9 + 9 + 3 = 29 → 2 + 9 = 11 → 1 + 1 = 2 (Moon / Chandra, with Master 11 noted).
  • Personal Year (Varshik Anka): Digital Root(Birth Day + Birth Month + Current Calendar Year), showing the active annual rhythm from 1 to 9.

Alongside those three core numbers, the calculator counts digit occurrences across your birth date to summarize present and Missing numbers (Open grid cells) in the 3x3 Lo Shu Grid (Lo Shu Square), and optionally checks your Calling Name number (Chaldean Name number) if you enter your name. Traditional planetary weekdays and colour palettes correspond to your Mulank's ruling planet (1 Sun, 2 Moon, 3 Guru / Jupiter, 4 Rahu / North Node, 5 Budh / Mercury, 6 Shukra / Venus, 7 Ketu / South Node, 8 Saturn, 9 Mars) for personal reflection and everyday habit planning, never as speculative or financial predictions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are my Favourable Numbers calculated? +
Your primary anchor is your Mulank (Birth Day number), obtained by reducing your calendar day of birth to 1 to 9. Your secondary anchor is your Bhagyank (Life Path number), obtained by reducing all digits of your full date of birth. Your active annual number comes from your Personal Year (Varshik Anka).
How can I use Favourable Numbers in everyday scheduling? +
In traditional numerology, people use their Favourable Numbers as simple personal preferences when picking calendar dates for reviews, choosing room or desk orientation, or selecting wardrobe colours—never for gambling, speculation, or promised outcomes.
What is the difference between Mulank and Bhagyank? +
Your Mulank (Birth Day number) comes solely from the day of the month you were born (1 to 31) and reflects everyday personal style. Your Bhagyank (Life Path number) sums day, month, and four-digit year together to reflect long-term life direction.
Do my Favourable Numbers change from year to year? +
Your Mulank (Birth Day number) and Bhagyank (Life Path number) remain constant for life because they come from your fixed birth date, while your Personal Year (Varshik Anka) advances by 1 each calendar year across a repeating 1-to-9 cycle.
How do Missing numbers in the Lo Shu Grid relate to Favourable Numbers? +
Plotting your birth-date digits into the 3x3 Lo Shu Grid (Lo Shu Square) highlights both present digits and Missing numbers (Open grid cells), showing which everyday habits complement your core Mulank and Bhagyank.
