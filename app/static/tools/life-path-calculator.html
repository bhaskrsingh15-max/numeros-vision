How Is Your Life Path Number Calculated?

Your Life Path Number is calculated from your full date of birth. The result depends on the numerology method selected.

Example: 15 June 1990

1
Day: 1 + 5 = 6
2
Month: June = 6
3
Year: 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
4
Total: 6 + 6 + 1 = 13 → 1 + 3 = 4
Result: Life Path Number 4

This example uses component reduction. Other numerology methods may handle intermediate numbers differently, so your calculator should clearly identify the method it actually uses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Life Path Number? +
A Life Path Number is a numerology number calculated from a person's date of birth using a specified method.
How is my Life Path Number calculated? +
The calculator applies its stated method to your birth date and shows the calculation steps.
Do Life Path Numbers have scientifically proven meanings? +
Numerology is a traditional interpretive practice. Its personality descriptions and predictions are not scientifically established facts.
Does the calculation method affect my result? +
It can. Different systems may use different reduction rules, so check which method the calculator follows.
What does a detailed Life Path report include? +
The contents depend on the report you select. Review the listed features before purchasing.
What are Master Numbers (11, 22, 33)? +
Master numbers are double digits (11, 22, 33) that carry amplified traditional symbolic significance and are preserved without immediate reduction in many traditions.

Yes. Calculating your number, viewing your calculation steps, and reading the initial interpretation are completely free. Extended multi-page reports are optional purchases.
