Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 | Jurisdiction: India (DPDP Act 2023) & Global (GDPR / CCPA)
🛡️

Zero-Fingerprint & AdTech Isolation Guarantee

Numeros Vision will never sell, rent, or monetize your sacred birth matrix, name inquiries, or personal spiritual questions to advertisers, data brokers, or advertising networks. All analytical processing is sandboxed through client-side hashing.

1. Introduction & Corporate Identity

Numeros Vision Consultancy ("Numeros Vision", "we", "us", or "our") operates the web platform at numerosvision.com and related mobile software applications. We are committed to safeguarding the digital personal data of every seeker, practitioner, and visitor in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India), the Information Technology Act, 2000, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

2. Categories of Data We Collect

In order to deliver Classical Calculation Precisionematical calculations, generated dossiers, and advisory sessions, we collect:

  • Sacred Birth Matrix Inputs: Full name (birth and active calling names), Date of Birth, Birth Time (for Birth Time Rectification), and City/Country of Birth.
  • Commercial & Consultation Inputs: Vehicle registration plates, mobile telephone numbers, proposed baby/business names, and partner birth details provided voluntarily for compatibility analysis.
  • Pseudonymous Technical Identifiers: We do not capture permanent hardware device fingerprints. A cryptographically salt-hashed session token (e.g. tok_...) is stored locally in your browser/device to maintain session continuity.
  • Payment & Invoicing Data: All payments are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways (Razorpay, Stripe). Numeros Vision does not store raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or net banking passwords. We receive only transaction status tokens, GSTINs (if provided), and confirmation receipts.

3. Lawful Grounds & Purposes of Processing

Under Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023 and Article 6 of the GDPR, your data is processed solely under the following lawful grounds:

  • Contractual Performance: To calculate your Core Matrix, Lo Shu grid planes, Dasha cycles, and generate your purchased Blueprint PDF report.
  • Explicit Consent: To connect you with an expert numerology consultant during live consultations or save chart preferences on your personal device.
  • Legal Compliance: To issue digital payment receipts, confirm service orders, and maintain financial records required under applicable Indian laws.

4. Prohibited Service Data & Data Broker Shield

We strictly distinguish between non-sensitive technical logs and Prohibited Service Data. The following data points are classified as sacred and strictly restricted:

  • Your exact date of birth, time of birth, and driver/conductor calculations.
  • Private consultation transcripts and audio/video recordings.
  • Specific health vitality tendencies and marital compatibility synastry.

This information is never aggregated into marketing profiles, never shared with programmatic ad exchanges, and never used to train public open-source foundational AI models.

5. Your Statutory Rights (DPDP Act & GDPR)

As a Data Principal, you possess unalienable rights regarding your digital footprint:

  • Right to Access & Summary: You may request a complete summary of personal data held by us along with the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: You may correct inaccurate or obsolete birth details stored in your profile at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may demand the complete deletion of your profile, session tokens, and generated reports from our primary databases.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw previously granted optional consents (such as anonymous analytics or regional localization) at any moment using our interactive Privacy Center below.
  • Right to Nominate: Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to nominate an individual who shall exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

6. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We employ multi-layered institutional safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • In Transit: All HTTP data is encrypted via modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with mandatory HSTS enforcement.
  • At Rest: Internal database archives and customer dossiers are protected with AES-256 bit encryption.
  • Application Firewall: Enterprise Web Application Firewall (WAF) filters SQL injection, Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), and automated scraping.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

Numeros Vision uses minimal, strictly essential browser storage to remember your numerological birth inputs so you do not need to re-type them when switching between tools. We do not place third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.
