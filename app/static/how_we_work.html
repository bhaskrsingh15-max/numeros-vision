The Four Core Traditions

Each tradition offers a unique lens on character, timing, and personal balance.

Root Vibrational Model

Chaldean tradition

The Chaldean system maps letter sounds and single-digit numbers to planetary archetypes. It focuses on your day of birth and compound totals, providing deep insight into inner motives, communication style, and growth edges.

Harmonic Cycles

Vedic tradition

The Vedic perspective explores natal planetary rulers, temporal Dasha cycles, and directional balance. It emphasizes pacing, personal duty, and aligning deliberate action with natural cycles of life.

Spatial & Elemental Matrix

Lo Shu grid

A classical 3×3 matrix that organizes your birth date across spatial coordinates and elemental energies. It maps balance across eight distinct life planes: three rows (Mental, Emotional, Practical), three columns (Thought, Will, Action), and two diagonals (Determination and Compassion).

Philosophical Archetypes

Pythagorean tradition

The foundational Western approach exploring the symbolic geometry of numbers 1 through 9. It provides an articulate comparative view of core personality polarities and collaborative dynamics.
