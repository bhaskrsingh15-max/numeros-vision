Rules: never stretch, recolour, crop letters, add rays or shadows, or re-type the tagline. Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the star. Spelling is always ANVEIL.

Sizes: circle logos and icons 4096 × 4096 px; header lockups 1024 px high (hd) and 2048 px high (2x).
