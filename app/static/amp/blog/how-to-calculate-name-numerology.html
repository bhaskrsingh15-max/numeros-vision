How to Calculate Name Numerology

A practical, manual step-by-step tutorial on calculating your name vibration using classical Chaldean sound acoustics, deciphering compound numbers, and achieving vibrational alignment.

📅 Published & Updated: November 5, 2026 ✦ Framework: Classical Chaldean Phonetics ✦ Focus: Practical & Mathematical Execution

Why Name Numerology Matters

While your date of birth represents an immutable blueprint (your Mulank or Driver number and Bhagyank or Conductor number), your name is a dynamic acoustic frequency. Every time your name is spoken, written, or heard, it generates an energetic resonance that influences social perception, career momentum, and personal harmony.

Classical Chaldean numerology assigns numeric values based on the sound frequency of each spoken letter. Here is how you can calculate it accurately by hand.

The 5 Steps to Calculate Name Numerology

STEP 1

Write Out Your Full Name in Block Capitals

Choose the name you wish to examine. For legal contracts, business identity, and passport audits, use your full legal name (First, Middle, Surname). For everyday social and professional analysis, evaluate the calling name by which people address you.

STEP 2

Substitute Each Letter with Its Chaldean Sound Value

Use the classical 1 to 8 Chaldean mapping table:

  • 1: A, I, J, Q, Y (Sun)
  • 2: B, K, R (Moon)
  • 3: C, G, L, S (Jupiter)
  • 4: D, M, T (Rahu)
  • 5: E, H, N, X (Mercury)
  • 6: U, V, W (Venus)
  • 7: O, Z (Ketu)
  • 8: F, P (Saturn)
  • Note: Number 9 is not assigned to any single letter in Chaldean numerology.
STEP 3

Sum the Digits for Each Word Separately

Calculate the sub-total for your first name, middle name, and last name independently before adding them together. The first name reflects your personal presentation and first impression; the surname reflects ancestral inheritance.

Name: ROHAN SHARMA
R(2) + O(7) + H(5) + A(1) + N(5) = 20
V(6) + E(5) + R(2) + M(4) + A(1) = 18
Total Compound Sum: 20 + 18 = 38
STEP 4

Examine the Compound Number (Sub-Vibration)

Before reducing the compound sum to a single digit, note the compound number itself (in this example, 38). In Chaldean tradition, compound numbers reveal hidden forces, karmic patterns, and specific life influences:

  • Highly Auspicious Compounds: 14, 19, 23, 24, 32, 37, 41, 46.
  • Compounds Requiring Conscious Caution: 12, 16, 18, 26, 28, 43.
STEP 5

Reduce to Single-Digit Root and Verify Harmony

Add the digits of the compound number together until a single digit between 1 and 9 is reached:

Compound: 38 -> 3 + 8 = 11 -> 1 + 1 = 2 (Moon Vibration)

Next, check the compatibility between this Name Root (2) and your Birth Driver/Conductor numbers. If your birth numbers are naturally friendly with Moon (such as 1, 3, or 5), the name flows harmoniously. If your birth number is in friction (such as 8 or 9), strategic spelling calibration can align the vibration.

Common Calculation Mistakes to Avoid

  • Mixing Pythagorean and Chaldean grids: Never use Pythagorean values (such as R=9 or S=1) with Chaldean rules.
  • Ignoring the Compound Number: Two names that both reduce to Root 5 may have very different compound vibrations (e.g., 23 vs 14 vs 32).
  • Over-reduction of Master Numbers: When calculating Pythagorean totals, numbers 11, 22, and 33 are traditionally preserved as master vibrations.
