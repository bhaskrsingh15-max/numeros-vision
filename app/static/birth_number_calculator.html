How the Birth Number (Mulank) is Calculated

The Birth Number is calculated by reducing the day of your birth to a single digit between 1 and 9 using basic digital root summation:

  • Single-digit days (1st to 9th): The birth number corresponds directly to that day (e.g. born on the 7th = Birth Number 7).
  • Double-digit days (10th to 31st): The two digits are added together until a single digit is reached:
    • Born on the 14th: \(1 + 4 = 5\) (Mercury)
    • Born on the 28th: \(2 + 8 = 10 \rightarrow 1 + 0 = 1\) (Sun)
    • Born on the 29th: \(2 + 9 = 11 \rightarrow 1 + 1 = 2\) (Moon)

Unlike the Life Path Number (which integrates the full birth date), your Birth Number reflects how you interact with the immediate world, your instinctive reactions, and how you assert your willpower.

The Nine Vedic Birth Archetypes

Every single-digit birth number is ruled by a primary celestial body in traditional Vedic Numerology:

  • Number 1 (Sun / Surya): Leadership, self-reliance, initiative, sovereign authority.
  • Number 2 (Moon / Chandra): Intuition, diplomatic grace, sensitivity, cooperative partnership.
  • Number 3 (Jupiter / Guru): Wisdom, expansive intellect, communication, counsel, optimism.
  • Number 4 (Rahu / North Node): Unconventional strategy, architectural mastery, order, innovation.
  • Number 5 (Mercury / Budh): Agility, analytical commercial acumen, communication, versatility.
  • Number 6 (Venus / Shukra): Harmony, aesthetic discernment, luxury, domestic care, magnetism.
  • Number 7 (Ketu / South Node): Research, analytical contemplation, spiritual inquiry, mysticism.
  • Number 8 (Saturn / Shani): Perseverance, karmic equilibrium, financial organization, stamina.
  • Number 9 (Mars / Mangal): Dynamic humanitarian courage, determination, universal empathy.
