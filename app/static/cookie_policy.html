TRANSPARENCY & DATA PRIVACY

Cookie Policy

Learn how Numeros Vision uses essential cookies and secure local browser storage to provide functional calculator sessions, verified payment tokens, and privacy preferences.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and local storage are small text files placed on your browser or device when you visit our website. They enable the platform to remember your active calculator session, your verified question credits, and your display preferences between visits.

2. Categories of Technologies We Use

  • Strictly Essential Cookies: Required to maintain secure HTTPS session tokens, CSRF protection, and Razorpay payment verification handshakes. These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional Local Storage: Temporarily stores your calculated birth numbers on your own device so you do not need to re-type your date of birth across multiple tools.
  • Zero Cross-Site Ad Tracking: We maintain a strict AdTech-free policy. We do NOT use third-party tracking pixels, cross-site advertising cookies, or behavioral data brokers.

3. Managing Your Browser Preferences

You can control or delete cookies directly through your web browser settings. Please note that disabling essential session cookies may prevent you from accessing verified paid reports or active Charm AI question passes.

For further information on our data governance practices, please read our complete Privacy Policy.
