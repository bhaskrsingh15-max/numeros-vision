Gemstone & Crystal Remedies
by Numerology Number

📅 Updated: November 5, 2026 ⏱ 10 min read ✦ Numeros Vision Research

In classical Classical planetary tradition (Ratna Shastra), every core number 1 through 9 is presided over by a sovereign planetary lord (Graha). Each celestial body emits specific cosmic rays that correspond to gemstone frequencies. Wearing the verified gemstone — on the designated finger, on the prescribed weekday, set in its matching metal — channels planetary grace and balances karmic lessons.

Crucial Practitioner Guidance: Potent gemstones — especially Blue Sapphire (Number 8) and Hessonite (Number 4) — should always be confirmed through complete birth chart cross-checking. Blue Sapphire mandates a 3-day touch trial before setting in metal.
