The Lo Shu Grid is a 4,000-year-old Chinese 3×3 magic square discovered on the shell of a sacred turtle emerging from the Luo River. Every horizontal row, vertical column, and diagonal sums precisely to 15. By mapping a person's date of birth into this sacred matrix, practitioners decipher 8 distinct life planes that reveal intellectual acumen, emotional resilience, material wealth, and real estate destiny.

Direct Answer: What are the 8 Planes of the Lo Shu Grid?

The 8 planes of the Lo Shu Grid are: (1) Mental Plane (4-9-2), (2) Emotional Plane (3-5-7), (3) Practical Plane (8-1-6), (4) Thought Plane (4-3-8), (5) Will Plane (9-5-1), (6) Action Plane (2-7-6), (7) Golden Rajyog (4-5-6), and (8) Silver Rajyog (2-5-8). Complete rows generate Arrows of Strength, while empty rows signify specific karmic blind spots.

1. The Three Horizontal Planes

✦ The Mental Plane (4 – 9 – 2)

Located at the top horizontal tier, this plane governs intellectual processing, memory retention, logical reasoning, and conceptual acuity. Those with all three digits (4, 9, 2) present possess an exceptional memory, grasp complex abstract theories effortlessly, and excel in academia, research, and high-stakes strategy.

✦ The Emotional / Spiritual Plane (3 – 5 – 7)

The central horizontal tier represents the human heart, intuition, emotional intelligence, and spiritual wisdom. When 3, 5, and 7 are all activated, the native enjoys profound empathy, psychic awareness, and the ability to heal and guide others through crisis.

✦ The Practical / Material Plane (8 – 1 – 6)

The bottom horizontal tier represents physical reality, grounded work, trade, and financial stewardship. Individuals carrying 8, 1, and 6 are masters of practical manifestation. They dislike idle daydreaming and excel at converting raw resources into commercial revenue.

3. The Two Sovereign Diagonals: Golden & Silver Rajyog

In Vedic and Indo-Chinese astro-numerology, the two diagonal alignments represent the most potent yogas of financial and material abundance:

👑 The Golden Rajyog (4 – 5 – 6)

Formed by Rahu (4), Mercury (5), and Venus (6), this diagonal is revered as the Supreme Line of Wealth and Success. Carriers of the full 4-5-6 combination possess natural administrative brilliance, charismatic social charm, and effortless capacity to accumulate luxury assets. Even if born in modest circumstances, they rarely face permanent financial distress.

🏛 The Silver Rajyog (2 – 5 – 8)

Formed along the opposite diagonal containing Moon (2), Mercury (5), and Saturn (8), all three numbers share the Earth element. This is recognized globally as the Property & Real Estate Plane. Natives with complete 2-5-8 patterns naturally acquire physical land, residential and commercial properties, and build lasting multi-generational wealth.
