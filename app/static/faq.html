What is the difference between my Birth Number and Life Path Number? ✦
Your Birth Number (Mulank) is calculated solely from the day of the month you were born (1–31 reduced to 1–9) and reveals your core instinctual nature and primary planetary archetype. Your Life Path Number (Bhagyank/Conductor) is calculated from your entire birth date (Day + Month + Year) and reveals your overarching life purpose, opportunities, and karmic trajectory.
