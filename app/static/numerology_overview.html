Explore Numerology and Its Different Methods

Numerology is an ancient symbolic framework that explores the qualitative resonance of numbers across human consciousness, time cycles, and personality archetypes. At Numeros Vision, we preserve the distinct integrity of each tradition without conflating their rules.

☀️ 1. Vedic Numerology (Ank Jyotish)

Vedic numerology is deeply linked to classical planetary archetypes (Navagraha). Every birth date is governed by two fundamental numbers:

  • Mulank (Driver / Birth Number): Calculated purely from the day of birth (1–31 reduced to 1–9). Represents your instinctual mind, inner character, and core personality traits.
  • Bhagyank (Conductor / Destiny Number): Calculated from the entire birth date (Day + Month + Year). Represents life's overarching path, outer circumstances, and soul trajectory.

In Vedic analysis, planetary relationships (friendship, neutrality, and enmity) determine how your Driver and Conductor numbers interact with each other.

📜 2. Chaldean Numerology

Originating in ancient Babylonia, the Chaldean system assigns numerical values from 1 to 8 to letters based on their acoustic vibration rather than English alphabetical sequence. The number 9 is considered sacred and held in reserve for spiritual completion.

Chaldean numerology places primary importance on compound numbers (from 10 to 52), which carry distinct esoteric symbolism (e.g., Number 23 'The Royal Star of the Lion' or Number 19 'The Prince of Heaven').

🔲 3. The Lo Shu Grid

The Lo Shu Grid is an ancient 3x3 magic square from Chinese cosmic geomancy where every row, column, and diagonal adds up to 15. In modern numerology, the digits of your birth date are placed onto the grid to examine the balance of 8 planes of consciousness:

  • Mental Plane (4, 9, 2): Intellectual clarity, memory, and cognitive discernment.
  • Emotional Plane (3, 5, 7): Compassion, intuition, spirituality, and artistic sense.
  • Practical Plane (8, 1, 6): Material mastery, discipline, and execution power.
  • Golden Rajyog (4, 5, 6): The plane of sovereign prosperity, resilience, and balance.
