Accessibility Statement

Numeros Vision is committed to ensuring that our digital numerology calculators, reports, and AI tools are accessible and barrier-free for individuals of all abilities.

1. Standards & Conformance

We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** standards. Our frontend architecture incorporates semantic HTML5, explicit ARIA roles, structured heading hierarchies (single H1 with logical H2/H3 nesting), and keyboard navigation across all interactive calculators and modal flows.

2. Visual Contrast & Color Harmony

In accordance with our design tokens, we deliberately avoid harsh pitch-black backgrounds and low-contrast grey text. Text is rendered in warm, high-contrast Jupiter Bronze (`#523E28`) on Venus Pearl surfaces (`#FFFDF7`), achieving a contrast ratio well in excess of the 4.5:1 requirement for standard body text.
